# Powietrze — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-17_

Powietrze is an air-quality app for Poland. It is built to need as little of
your data as possible. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does
not do with information.

## Short version

- **No account. No sign-up. No ads. No tracking. No third-party analytics.**
- Your **location never leaves your device**.
- The only server the app contacts is the public Polish air-quality API,
  **GIOŚ** (`api.gios.gov.pl`).
- Your favourites and settings are stored **only on your device**.

## Location

If you enable "Użyj mojej lokalizacji", the app asks iOS for your location so it
can show the measuring station nearest to you. Your coordinates are used
**entirely on the device**: the app downloads the public list of GIOŚ stations
and calculates the closest one locally. **Your coordinates are never sent to us
or to any third party, and are never stored off your device.** You can use the
app without location by choosing a place manually; the app then defaults to
Kraków.

## Data we collect

**None that leaves your device.** Powietrze has no backend server, no user
accounts, and collects no personal data. We do not use advertising or analytics
SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

## Data stored on your device

- Your saved places (favourites) and app settings, kept locally so the app
  remembers them between launches. Removing the app removes this data.

## Network requests

The app requests publicly available air-quality measurements from GIOŚ (Główny
Inspektorat Ochrony Środowiska), the Polish Chief Inspectorate of Environmental
Protection, at `api.gios.gov.pl`. These requests identify a measuring **station**
(by its public ID) — never you. Standard technical information (such as your IP
address) is visible to that service as with any internet request; that exchange
is governed by GIOŚ's own terms.

## Notifications

If you enable notifications, the app schedules **local** reminders on your device
(for example, the morning summary). These are generated on the device — there is
no push server and no message is sent to or from us.

## Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any
data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
